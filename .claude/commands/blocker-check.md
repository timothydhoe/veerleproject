Read `CLAUDE.md` (the Open Blockers section) and `config.yaml`, then produce a short
briefing that could be handed to a non-technical researcher before a meeting.

Structure it as three sections:

**What we're still waiting for**
List everything that is blocking the analysis from completing. For each item, give one
sentence explaining *why* it matters — what breaks or can't run without it. Be specific:
name the schools, the parameters, the decisions still needed.

**What we can already do**
List what parts of the pipeline can run right now, even with the current gaps. Be honest
about limitations — e.g. "the pipeline can run but won't classify activity correctly
until cut-points are confirmed."

**Questions to raise**
Turn the blockers into specific questions, phrased as if you're asking Veerle directly.
For example: "Do you have the ENMO cut-point values from citation 7 in your protocol?"
or "Can you send the confirmed timetable for School 3?"

Keep the whole thing to one page. No bullet points longer than two lines. No technical
jargon.
